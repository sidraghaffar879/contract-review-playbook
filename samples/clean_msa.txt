MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

1. Fees and Payment
Customer shall pay all invoices within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Late payments accrue interest at 1% per month.

2. Term and Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on thirty (30) days written notice, or for material breach not cured within thirty (30) days written notice.

3. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential for three (3) years after termination.

4. Limitation of Liability
Each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed the fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months, except for breach of confidentiality, IP infringement or gross negligence.

5. Indemnification
Each party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims that the indemnifying party's materials infringe intellectual property rights.

6. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, and the parties submit to the courts of London.

7. Data Protection
Each party shall comply with GDPR and notify the other of any personal data breach within seventy-two (72) hours.
